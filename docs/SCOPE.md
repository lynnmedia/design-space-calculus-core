# Scope and evidence limits

This preview covers representation, compilation, conformance validation, controlled failure, provenance in canonical IR and the preservation ledger, minimal workspace initialization, and the bounded `novum-dsc/0.1` importer. The exact public source list is the versioned release tree and its file-level license map.

It excludes experimental search control, generative expansion, model providers, scoring, Pareto selection, historical research artifacts, private IIF records, and held-out trial materials. It makes no claim that DSC improves solution quality, novelty, invention yield, or real-world productivity.

IIE-009 tested a frozen prompt scaffold, not this RC1 software package. It did not support a Solution Quality, novelty, or transformation-operator advantage. This candidate makes no claim from that trial's Epistemic Integrity result. Current RC1 interoperability qualification is a software correctness result, not efficacy or full semantic-equivalence evidence. Historical IIE-005 and IIE-GEN-001 findings do not establish current RC1 efficacy. Generalization across models, interactive workflows, human collaboration, and real-world productivity is untested.

No raw trial inputs, outputs, judgments, blind keys, or private control-plane records are included. No public citation target for those private records is asserted here.
